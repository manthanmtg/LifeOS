# Expense Spaces

Use Expense Spaces for costs that deserve their own ledger, budget, and
reporting—for example a house renovation, a trip, pet care, or estate work.
Your regular **Expenses** module stays separate.

## Start a space

1. Choose **New space** and give it a clear, unique name.
2. Pick the currency before entering expenses. Currency is locked after the
   first entry so historical amounts cannot be relabeled accidentally.
3. Add an optional total or monthly budget.
4. Use the provided **Other** category immediately, or create your own category
   and subcategory structure in **Settings**.

## Record expenses

Open a space and choose **Add expense**. Amount, date, description, paid to,
and category are required. You can also record a subcategory, payment method,
reference, notes, tags, and receipt URL.

Need a category while entering an expense? Create it inline. LifeOS saves the
category first, then the expense. If the expense save fails, your draft remains
available to retry.

Use ledger filters to search and narrow by dates, category, subcategory, payee,
or payment method. Pages are sorted newest first by default.

## Categories and history

Rename or archive a used category instead of deleting it. Archived taxonomy is
hidden from new entries but stays attached to historical records and analytics.
Unused categories and subcategories can be removed.

## Analytics

The **Analytics** tab summarizes spend by category, category/subcategory path,
paid to, payment method, and month, with totals and largest expenses. Date
presets include All time, This month, This year, and Custom.

The all-spaces view always asks for a currency. LifeOS does not perform currency
conversion and never combines incompatible amounts into one total. Archived
spaces remain included so archiving does not erase history from reports.

## Archive or delete

Archive a finished space to make it read-only while keeping it discoverable and
included in reporting. Restore it before adding or changing entries. Hard
delete removes the space and all of its entries, requires typing the exact
space name, and cannot be undone through LifeOS.
