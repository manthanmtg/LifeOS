# Health

A centralized vault for medical history, prescriptions, vaccinations, and health tracking for family members and pets.

## Features

- **Multi-Profile Tracking**: Manage health data for yourself, family members, or pets in one place.
- **Smart Health Insights**: Automatically identifies overdue vaccinations and upcoming medication refills.
- **Comprehensive Record Keeping**:
  - **Conditions & Allergies**: Keep track of chronic issues and allergic reactions.
  - **Medication Management**: Log dosage, prescriber, and refill schedules.
  - **Vaccination Timeline**: History of doses and automated booster alerts.
  - **Visit Log**: Record doctor consultations, diagnoses, and costs.
  - **Lab Results**: Log clinical data with status color-coding.
  - **Physical Stats**: Track height, weight, and BMI trends.
  - **Document Vault**: Attach prescriptions, bills, and lab reports.

## How to Use

1. **Create Profiles**: Add a profile for each family member or pet.
2. **Backfill Records**: Add existing medical history, current medications, and vaccination dates.
3. **Log Activity**: As events happen, record doctor visits and new lab results.
4. **Monitor Trends**: Use the Body Stats and Lab Results tabs to watch for changes over time.

## Tips

- **Keep it Updated**: Update medication refill dates so you get timely dashboard alerts.
- **Log Booster Dates**: Record the 'Next Due' date for vaccinations to stay current.
- **Scan Paperwork**: Use the Documents tab to store digital copies of medical bills and prescriptions.
